Apply a small amount of optical-grade lens cleaning solution to a microfiber, lint-free cloth. Do not apply liquid directly to the lens. Using a gentle, circular motion, wipe the lens from the center outward to remove oils, fingerprints, or atmospheric deposits.

3.3 Post-Cleaning Verification
