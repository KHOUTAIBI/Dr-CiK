2.2 Thermal Paper Specifications
All hardcopy output must utilize approved internal thermal paper to ensure print head longevity and legibility.
- Material Type: High-sensitivity Archive-Grade Thermal Paper
- Coating: Dual-layer heat-sensitive chemical coating
- Paper Thickness: Standard receipt printing thickness is calibrated to exactly 65 microns (±2 microns). This specific thickness is required to ensure consistent feeding through the internal roller mechanism and clean separation at the tear-bar.

3. MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES

3.1 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Clearing Internal Paper Jams
Staff must follow these protocols to prevent damage to the thermal headers:
1. Power down the printer interface via the module maintenance sub-panel.
2. Open the primary access hatch using the FEMD-issue key.
3. Identify the jam location (Roller Gap A or Feed Path B).
4. Gently rotate the manual feed tensioner counter-clockwise to release paper tension.
5. Remove obstructed paper using non-conductive tweezers; do not use metal blades.
6. Clear the internal optical sensor with a compressed air canister.
7. Re-thread the 65-micron thermal paper and perform a test feed.

3.2 Staff Training Protocols
Maintenance personnel must complete the 'Module-Echo Printer Handling' certification. Training includes manual paper threading, thermal head cleaning, and the identification of ink cartridge depletion markers. Refresher training is required bi-annually.
